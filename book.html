Honey the Horse Series · Book One

Honey Helps Me Feel Calm

Healing hooves, one heart at a time.

Big feelings can sometimes feel overwhelming.
But Honey knows how to stay calm.

Inspired by a real horse named Honey, this gentle and comforting story follows a child learning to feel safe, steady, and understood through the quiet presence of a kind horse. With soothing watercolor illustrations and calming emotional themes inspired by the healing connection between horses and people, Honey Helps Me Feel Calm encourages children to slow down, breathe deeply, and discover that calm can grow inside their own hearts.

A heartwarming story about emotions, connection, comfort, and healing hooves — one heart at a time.
