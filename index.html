Honey Helps Me Feel Calm

Healing hooves, one heart at a time.

Get the Book A Note for Parents

Big feelings can sometimes feel overwhelming.

But Honey knows how to stay calm. Inspired by a real horse named Honey, this gentle story follows a child learning to feel safe, steady, and understood through the quiet presence of a kind horse. With soothing watercolor illustrations, Honey Helps Me Feel Calm invites children to slow down, breathe deeply, and discover that calm can grow inside their own hearts too.

Meet the real Honey

Every story in this series is inspired by a real horse named Honey — gentle, patient, and full of quiet wisdom. Her calm nature, and the way children naturally settle in her presence, is the heart of every page.
