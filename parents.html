For Parents & Caregivers

A Note for Parents

Many children experience big feelings in different ways. Some may feel worried, overwhelmed, sensitive, or unsure how to express what's happening inside.

Horses are deeply sensitive animals, known for noticing subtle changes in human body language, breathing, and emotional state. Equine-assisted therapy and horse-based emotional support programs are used around the world to help children build confidence, emotional regulation, and connection in gentle, supportive environments.

As you share this story with your child

  • Read slowly and enjoy the quiet moments together.
  • Notice Honey's calm body language and slow breathing.
  • Ask your child how they think the child in the story is feeling.
  • Talk gently about times when feelings have felt big.
  • Allow your child to reflect and respond in their own time.
While this story isn't intended as therapy or medical advice, it was created to reflect the quiet comfort, safety, and emotional connection many children experience around horses. May Honey's story remind children that they are safe, supported, and never alone in their feelings.
